# Legacy Trunk
Legacy Trunk is a secure digital platform dedicated to preserving and celebrating your family’s legacy. It empowers family members to capture, share, and relive stories, photos, and cherished memories in one organized space. With features like interactive timelines, smart tagging, private family circles, collaborative editing, and advanced AI-powered tools, Legacy Trunk ensures that your family’s traditions, milestones, and heritage are safeguarded and effortlessly passed down to future generations.
## Problem Description
The platform solves the challenge of preserving family history digitally while maintaining privacy. It allows members to contribute stories, create timelines, tag memories, collaborate on content, and securely share within private family circles.

## 🛠 Features

### Core Features
- **Family Story Recording** – Upload text, photo, audio, or video stories
- **Timeline Creation** – Chronological mapping of life events
- **Photo Uploads with Tagging** – Add descriptions, tags, and dates
- **Private Family Circles** – Secure sharing with selected members
- **Search Functionality** – Search by keyword, tag, or media type
- **User Authentication & Roles** – Manage contributors (parents, grandparents, kids)
- **Story Export** – Generate PDFs or books from stories

### Advanced Features
- **Memory Prompts** – Predefined templates to encourage storytelling
- **AI Text Analysis** – Auto-tagging & categorization
- **Cross-Generational Matching** – Suggest stories of similar themes across generations
- **Collaborative Story Editing** – Multiple contributors co-create a story
- **Customizable Calendar** – Public/private reminders
- **Family History Chatbot** – Query family stories & people
- **Sharable Prompts** – Share prompts for others to respond
  
##  External Libraries & APIs
- **Frontend:** React.js, TailwindCSS  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Socket.IO  
- **Authentication:** JWT, bcrypt.js  
- **File Storage:** Cloudinary
- **Exporting:** pdf-lib / jsPDF
- **AI:** Gemini API
